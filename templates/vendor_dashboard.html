{{ icon('bell', 20) }} Never miss an order 3 quick steps {{ icon('chevron-right', 18) }}
  1. 1
    Turn on order notifications

    A pop-up with your phone's sound for every new order, even when Katale is closed. It rings again after 1 and 2 minutes until you tap it.

  2. 2
    Install Katale on your phone

    Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
    iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On iPhone, notifications only work from the installed app.

  3. 3
    Give Katale a loud sound of its own

    Android: Settings → Apps → Katale → Notifications → Sound → pick a loud tone (an alarm tone works well).
    iPhone: Settings → Notifications → Katale → turn on Sounds.
    At the counter: keep this page open with Turn on sound pressed. New orders ring until someone taps them.
